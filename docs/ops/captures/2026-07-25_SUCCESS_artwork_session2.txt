## Why this needed reassembly

A 65535-byte iAP2 link packet becomes a 65567-byte RCS message. The crypto frame
length field is u16 and the DataStream frames at 16384 bytes, so such a message
always spans multiple frames. Parsing each frame in isolation dropped every piece:
only Setup (42 B) and the final fragment (15852 B) fit one frame and parsed, so the
transfer never completed. drain_rcs() now accumulates by totalLength.
